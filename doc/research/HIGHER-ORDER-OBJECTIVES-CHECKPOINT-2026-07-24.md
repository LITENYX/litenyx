1. Serve real transaction demand.

3. Expand through demand-driven, profit-qualified segmentation.
4. Preserve required security and a minimum economically meaningful fork/attack budget for every economically significant active domain.
5. Support economically viable inter-chain activity.
6. Remain adaptable as demand and mining/security conditions change.

- Demand decides WHERE to scale.
